Estos datos van unidos a tu cuenta aunque no incluyan tu nombre real. Los ven tus amigos y quien tenga tu código de amigo; quien hayas bloqueado no puede abrir tu perfil. Las denuncias que se hagan se guardan para moderar la comunidad. Si no usas estas funciones, nada de esto se crea.

If you use Friends, the Arena or challenges, RivalWord creates a Firebase (Google) account —without requiring an email or password, or linked to your Apple ID if you choose “Sign in with Apple”— and stores on its servers the name you choose, your learning language, your friend code, your friendships, requests and blocks, your crowns, your duels and the challenges you send or receive (with both players' names and scores), and which words you've practised, so it can pick questions you've both studied. This data is linked to your account even though it doesn't include your real name. Your friends and anyone with your friend code can see it; people you block can't open your profile. Reports are kept to moderate the community. If you don't use these features, none of this is created.

Сигналите се пазят за модериране на общността. Ако не използваш тези функции, нищо от това не се създава.
